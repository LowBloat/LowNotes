# Code of conduct

LowNotes adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The summary below does not replace the full text.

## Our pledge

We want participation in LowNotes to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and kind, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at contato@fabioharaujo.com.br. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove contributions and restrict participation, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
